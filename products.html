Product Specifications & Care Guide

Pure Materials & Fabrics

Every single fabric and ingredient touching your baby's skin is non-toxic, hypoallergenic, and ethically sourced.

  • 100% GOTS Organic Cotton: Free of formaldehyde, bleach & heavy metals.
  • Natural Bamboo Fiber: Hypoallergenic, thermal-regulating & moisture wicking.
  • Food-Grade Silicone: BPA-free, PVC-free, phthalate-free and lead-free.
  • Botanical Skincare: Calendula, chamomile, shea butter & colloidal oat.

Care & Usage Guidelines

Follow these simple laundering and storage guidelines to preserve fabric softness and safety.

  • Washing Apparel: Machine wash cold on gentle cycle with plant-based detergent.
  • Drying: Tumble dry low or line dry in shaded area to protect fibers.
  • Skincare Application: Patch test 24 hours prior to first full-body routine.
  • Feeding Sanitizing: Boil glass bottles 5 minutes prior to initial use.
